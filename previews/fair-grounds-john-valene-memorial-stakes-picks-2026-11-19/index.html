John Valene Memorial Picks: Little Miss Curlin vs. the Brad Cox Favorite

A 3-year-old with seven wins from 10 starts. A Brad Cox mare dropping out of Grade 1 company. And an Asmussen speedster who just missed last time.

The John Valene Memorial is a $100,000 stakes for Louisiana-bred fillies and mares at one mile and 70 yards on the Fair Grounds dirt, and only six go to post. Every one of them has won over this track, and the top three all come in with real form.

The morning line makes Margie's Intention the clear favorite. Our ratings lean the other way, toward a 3-year-old who keeps winning. Here's how I see it.

#6 · 7-2Little Miss Curlin

Record10: 7-0-1Earnings$428,902Last startWon at Delta Downs, Oct 9SireCharlatanMorning line7-2

Little Miss Curlin tops our ratings. She has won seven of her 10 starts and has won three times over this track. Last time out she took a big step forward, winning the Magnolia Stakes at Delta Downs on Oct. 9.

Patrick Devereux Jr. does well with dirt runners, she has trained well, and J. Loveberry rides. The question is distance: her recent races have all been sprints, and this is a route. Her pedigree says she can handle it, but she has to prove it.

#4 · 8-5 favoriteMargie's Intention

Record13: 4-4-2Earnings$581,880Last start5th at Delaware, Oct 3SireHonor A. P.Morning line8-5

Margie's Intention is clearly the fastest horse in the field on recent figures, and she drops in class after two tries in graded stakes: 5th in the Grade 1 Personal Ensign at Saratoga and 5th in the Grade 3 Delaware Handicap. In May she won a listed stakes at Laurel.

Brad Cox does well with horses coming back in a month or two, and A. Concepcion rides. At 8-5 she's the logical horse, but she hasn't won since the spring, and those last two races weren't close.

#2 · 3-1Blue Fire

Record16: 6-6-0Earnings$573,318Last start2nd at Churchill, Sep 11SireAurelius MaximusMorning line3-1

Blue Fire has the second-best recent figures in the field and has won six times over this track. She just missed in an optional claimer at Churchill last time, and she has run well at this kind of distance.

She's the main speed, along with Kalil, and she returns from about two months off. Steve Asmussen trains and P. Lopez rides. If she gets loose on the lead, she can carry it a long way.

#3 · 9-2Braken Poppa

Record6: 4-1-0Earnings$196,200Last start7th at Laurel, May 15SireAurelius MaximusMorning line9-2

Braken Poppa is 4-for-6 and won two stakes over this track last winter. She was 7th in the Grade 2 Black-Eyed Susan in May and returns from about six months off. She's also an Asmussen runner, and she sits just off the pace, which could be exactly where you want to be if Blue Fire and Kalil go at it.

#1 · 6-1Moonie Pie

Record14: 4-4-4Earnings$179,310Last start4th at Evangeline, Aug 31SireDialed InMorning line6-1

Moonie Pie has hit the board in five of her last six, and her 4th in a stakes at Evangeline in August was closer than it looked, beaten just 2 lengths. She returns from about three months off but has worked sharply since.

Race shapeHow the Race Sets Up

Blue Fire and Kalil figure to go after the lead together, and in a six-horse field there's nowhere to hide. Whichever settles better should hold on longer.

That sets up for a filly sitting just behind them. Braken Poppa is the stalker our ratings point to, with Little Miss Curlin and Margie's Intention close by.
