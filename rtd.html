The rules

How horses qualify for the Kentucky Derby

  1. The top five finishers in each points race earn points. The bigger the race and the closer to May, the more it's worth.
  2. The top 17 on the final leaderboard get a spot. Three more spots are held for invitees: up to two from the Euro/Middle East series and one from Japan. Any invitation that isn't used goes to the next horse on the American leaderboard.
  3. Ties are broken by earnings in non-restricted stakes races, then lifetime earnings, then a draw.
  4. Up to four also-eligibles wait in line in case a horse in the field drops out before the scratch deadline.

New to the Derby trail? 8 terms in plain English
Points race (prep race)
A race on the official Road to the Kentucky Derby. Only the top five finishers earn points.
Championship Series
The 16 biggest points races, from mid-February to mid-April. Most pay 50 or 100 points to the winner.
Leaderboard
The running points total for every horse. The final leaderboard in April sets the Derby field.
Also-eligible
A horse just outside the 20 who draws into the race if someone scratches before the deadline.
Grade (G1, G2, G3)
The class of a stakes race. Grade 1 is the top level.
Synthetic
An all-weather surface instead of dirt. Turfway Park's points races are run on it.
Triple Crown nomination
To run in the Derby, a horse must be nominated to the Triple Crown. Late nominations cost more.
Future Wager
A bet on the Derby placed months ahead at fixed odds. No refunds if your horse doesn't run.

Quick answers

Road to the Kentucky Derby FAQ

When is the 2027 Kentucky Derby?

Saturday, May 1, 2027, at Churchill Downs in Louisville. The Kentucky Oaks is the day before, Friday, April 30.

Who leads the Road to the Kentucky Derby?

Hughes, trained by Bob Baffert, with 40 points. American History is second with 25 and Salahudin third with 19.

When is the next Kentucky Derby prep race?

The Kentucky Jockey Club at Churchill Downs on Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026. It pays 10-5-3-2-1 points to the top five.

How many horses get into the Kentucky Derby on points?

The top 17 on the American leaderboard. Three more spots are held for invitees, up to two from the Euro/Middle East series and one from Japan, and any spot that isn't used goes to the next American horse. The field is capped at 20.

How many points does a horse need to make the Kentucky Derby?

There's no fixed number. It depends on how the season plays out and how many horses drop out. In 2026, the last horses into the field had 25 points.

Which prep races are worth the most points?

The Louisiana Derby, Jeff Ruby Steaks, Florida Derby, Santa Anita Derby, Blue Grass, Wood Memorial and Arkansas Derby each pay 100-50-25-15-10.

How many points have Kentucky Derby winners had?

Since the points system began in 2013, Authentic had the most (200, in 2020) and Rich Strike the fewest (21, in 2022). Rich Strike only got in from the also-eligible list.

What happens if horses are tied on points?

The tiebreaker is earnings in non-restricted stakes races, then lifetime earnings. If they're still tied, it's decided by a draw.

Can a filly run in the Kentucky Derby?

Yes. She has to earn her way in with points from Road to the Kentucky Derby races, the same as the colts.
